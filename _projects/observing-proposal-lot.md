<h3>Abstract</h3>

This observing proposal aimed to conduct a broad taxonomic survey of asteroids with dynamically unstable orbits in the outer main asteroid belt. These objects may have originated from cometary sources or been scattered from the main belt because of gravitational perturbations from Jupiter or non-gravitational effects. The proposal anticipated a bimodal color distribution associated with different source regions and thermal histories, including C-type material related to wetter compositions and S-type silicate surfaces. By examining the ratio of these populations, the project helps constrain transport models within the Solar System and improve our understanding of the orbital evolution pathways of dynamically unstable orphan objects.

The observing campaign led to the discovery of weak cometary activity on the outer main-belt asteroid 2005 XR132 on 4 May 2021.
