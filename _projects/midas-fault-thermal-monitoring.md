<h3>Abstract</h3>

This project focuses on borehole optical fiber sensing and distributed temperature sensing to investigate the thermal characteristics of the Milun fault. The research aims to identify persistent thermal anomalies, understand subsurface structure, and characterize fault-zone properties through continuous borehole temperature observations.
