<h3>Chemical Composition of the Inner Coma of Comet 67P - Rosetta Mission</h3>

<h4>Abstract</h4>

The Rosetta mission greatly improved our understanding of cometary science through its long-term observations of comet 67P from 2014 to 2016. In this project, we studied the evolution of coma gas composition using Rosetta MIRO data. We also investigated the outgassing activity associated with solar illumination effects, including day-night asymmetry and heliocentric-distance dependence, to better understand the controlling processes in subsurface ice sublimation and gas dynamics in the inner coma.

This work also examined the chemical composition of the coma to improve understanding of heterogeneity in the nucleus ices. In addition, the MIRO data were compared with outburst events detected by Rosetta OSIRIS in order to investigate dust-gas interactions.
