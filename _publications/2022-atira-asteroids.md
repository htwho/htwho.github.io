<h3>Abstract</h3>

Asteroids having perihelion distance q < 1.3 AU are classified as near-Earth objects (NEOs), which are divided into different sub-groups: Vatira-class, Atira-class, Aten-class, Apollo-class, and Amor-class. 2020 AV2, the first Vatira, was discovered by the Twilight project of the Zwicky Transient Facility on 2020 January 4. In this work, the long-term orbital evolution of known near-Earth objects and known Atiras under the Yarkovsky effect was assessed by using the Mercury6 N-body code. The calculations considered both planetary gravitational perturbations and the non-gravitational Yarkovsky effect. The results suggest that NEOs have two dynamical populations, one short-lived and the other long-lived, and that radiation forces may play a role in the long-term evolution of the Atira population. The statistical analysis further suggests that there should be 8.14 +/- 0.133 Atira-class asteroids and 1.05 +/- 0.075 Vatira asteroids of the S-type taxonomy.
