<h3>Abstract</h3>

The official proceedings entry confirms this paper as a conference contribution in the *18th Annual Meeting of the Asia Oceania Geosciences Society* proceedings. Based on the paper title and the corresponding research line developed by the authors, this work discusses the dynamical lifetime and orbital evolution of near-Earth objects under the influence of the Yarkovsky effect, with emphasis on how non-gravitational thermal forces affect long-term transport and survival in near-Earth space.
